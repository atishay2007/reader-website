The Journey

A life built line by line, in service of the Hindi language

2000
Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut

M.A. Hindi — Gold Medalist

Graduated at the top of her class, awarded the Gold Medal in Hindi — the first mark of a career defined by scholarly distinction.

2003
M.J.P. Rohilkhand University, Bareilly

Ph.D. in Journalism

Doctoral thesis, "Journalism in Uttar Pradesh: Achievements and Evaluation," laying the foundation for a lifelong inquiry into language and media.

2011
Maharshi Dayanand University

B.Ed.

Formal training in pedagogy, sharpening a growing devotion to the classroom and the next generation of Hindi readers and writers.

Cyprus Years
Founder, Cyprus NRI Association & Abhivyakti

Carrying Hindi Abroad

Founded the Cyprus NRI Association and launched Abhivyakti, a publication built to keep the Hindi language alive within the diaspora — work that would later inspire her book Cyprus Nahin Lubhata.

3 Years
Pawar Public School, Pune (CBSE)

Hindi Teacher

Taught Hindi to middle and secondary students, receiving formal recognition for outstanding academic contribution to the institution.

3 Years
Amanora School, Pune (CBSE)

Head of Department — Hindi

Led the Hindi department, shaping curriculum and academic planning while mentoring students across literary and co-curricular programmes.

2021
Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar University, Solapur

M.A. Prakrit

Returned to formal study, deepening her command of classical language traditions that inform her literary criticism.

Ongoing
Maharashtra Hindi Sahitya Academy

Pratidhwani & Continued Publication

Her book Pratidhwani, supported by the Maharashtra Hindi Sahitya Academy, along with essays across national journals and three stories selected for NCERT supplementary readers.

Present
Pune, India

Ph.D. Research Guide, Applicant

Now guiding the next generation of scholars — carrying forward more than two decades in Hindi academia into research mentorship.

Published Works

Three books, each a chapter of a wandering, devoted life

Travel & Reflection

Cyprus Nahin Lubhata

Born of years spent founding the Cyprus NRI Association and publishing Abhivyakti abroad, this work traces the pull of home against the quiet enchantments of a foreign island.

Book
Poetry & Prose

Laharon Ki Muskan

A collection that moves like water — meditative, lyrical, and unhurried — reflecting the author's long relationship with Hindi verse and the everyday poetry of memory.

Book
Literary Fiction

Pratidhwani

Supported by the Maharashtra Hindi Sahitya Academy, Pratidhwani — "the echo" — is a resonant literary work honouring language as a vessel that carries voices forward.
